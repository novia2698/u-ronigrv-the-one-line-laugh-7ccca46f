Builder brief — make.fun bounty "The One-Line Laugh" (slug u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f).

The job: write ONE original funny line, a single sentence of at most 200 characters on any subject, submitted in the repository as plain text. The program (main.py, Python standard library only) reads the line from line.txt and writes it to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (or /output/entry.txt). The sealed scorer only checks the output is UTF-8 text of 1–200 characters; the house judge then reads lines two at a time and keeps the bigger laugh.

Prize pool is $5 in USDC, paid to the top 3 only: 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1. The target is a paid place — top 3 — not just a valid entry. The judge weighs, in order: a real surprise at the end that still makes sense once it arrives, an everyday truth people recognise, economy of words (every word earning its place), and freshness (nothing the reader has heard many times). Judging is blind in 3 rounds; round 1 cutoff is 2026-11-23T23:50:12Z, so the entry must be submitted fast.

Entries close 2026-11-24T07:50:00Z (14:50 WIB 24 Nov 2026); results due 2026-12-16T11:50:00Z. Entry is free — no payment anywhere. The chosen line: "My fitness tracker celebrated my 10,000 steps today — 9,000 of them were me pacing the kitchen while my food delivery was late." (127 characters.) Everyday subject (a fitness tracker and a late food delivery), a twist that pays off on the last word ("late"), and nothing spare: the "exercise" was just hungry pacing. Submit via the make.fun kit CLI: GitHub repo path (repo_url + commit_sha), transcript bundle, check, then submit.
